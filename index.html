Questions

Do I need Excel?

No. The files work in Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer and in Google Sheets, which is free. In Google Sheets, use File → Import or upload the file to Drive and open it.

Will it work on my phone?

You can view and update the files in the Google Sheets or Excel app. Setting up your prices is easiest on a computer the first time.

I'm not good with spreadsheets. Is this for me?

Yes. Each file opens on a "Start here" tab with step-by-step instructions. You only type in the yellow cells; everything else calculates for you.

Are the prices in the calculator right for my area?

They're examples. You set your own rates, multipliers and add-on prices on the Rates tab, and the calculator uses yours.

Is the bookkeeping tracker tax advice?

No. It's a record-keeping tool that organizes your income, expenses and mileage so year-end is easier. Share the summary with your tax preparer.

Can I get a refund?

Because these are instant digital downloads, sales are final. If a file doesn't open or something's broken, email us and we'll fix it or refund you.

Can I use these for my team?

Yes, within your own business. Please don't resell or share the files publicly.
